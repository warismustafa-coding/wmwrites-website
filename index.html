Who We Are

WMwrites

WMwrites is an independent, non-governmental organization founded on March 1, 2026, by Waris Mustafa. The organization focuses on two main areas: Writing and Publication. WMwrites aims to develop clear, structured, and accessible academic content based on learners’ needs. Through needs analysis, content development, and systematic review, the organization works to address gaps in learning and help readers understand concepts rather than simply memorize facts. Its approach is built on the belief that a strong understanding of ideas can last longer than the memorization of written details. Beyond academic publication, WMwrites recognizes writing as a way to express personal experiences, explore ideas, and communicate insights from mental, social, psychological, and practical perspectives. Through its work, WMwrites seeks to support learners and readers by providing reliable, meaningful, and academically appropriate resources that make learning more understandable and accessible.

About Us

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

The vision of WMwrites is to establish itself as a dependable and reputable platform for practical writing and academic content. It aims to be a place learners can rely on, where they can access clear, concept-based material that supports sustained comprehension and meaningful educational experiences. The goal is to make learning last.

Our Mission

The mission of WMwrites has two main focuses. First, it aims to develop structured content that puts learners first, with an emphasis on clarity, real understanding, and accessibility. Second, it supports writing that matters in practical life. This includes bringing together personal experience, social perspectives, and the lessons gained through experience.

Message from the Founder and CEO

Message from Waris Mustafa

At WMwrites, my vision is to make quality educational resources accessible to learners and to support the development of knowledge through academic publications, research, and educational material development. I founded WMwrites with the belief that education can create meaningful opportunities when knowledge is presented clearly, thoughtfully, and in a way that meets learners' needs.

With an academic background in Applied Linguistics, I have a strong interest in language, literature, and education. Through WMwrites, I aim to bring together individuals who share a commitment to learning, academic growth, and the development of valuable educational resources. Our work currently focuses on two areas: Linguistics and Literature, with the goal of developing materials that support students and contribute to these fields.

As Founder and Chief Executive Officer, I am committed to building WMwrites into a platform where ideas can develop into meaningful educational projects through collaboration, careful review, and a shared sense of responsibility. I believe that sustainable progress comes from dedication, teamwork, and a clear purpose. My long-term vision is to help WMwrites grow into an organization that contributes positively to education and creates opportunities for people to share their knowledge and expertise.

Message from the Chief Advisor

Message from Aisha Bint-e-Siraj

As an English language educator, academic leader, public speaking trainer, and debate adjudicator, I bring six years of teaching experience to my work. I currently serve as the Academic Coordinator of O Levels at The City School, Jinnah Campus, Hyderabad. I hold a BS in English with a specialisation in Applied Linguistics from the University of Sindh, Jamshoro, where I ranked at the top of my class for four consecutive years.

As a Cambridge-certified O Level and A Level English instructor, I have gained extensive experience in academic coordination, curriculum implementation, student leadership, debating, and communication training. I am an alumna of the Pakistan-U.S. Alumni Network, a certified English Access MicroScholarship Program and English Works! teacher, an American English Live Series 17 Ambassador, and a participant in the Professional Development for Global Classrooms (PGCC) 2025 programme. I have also led educational initiatives, including the ChangeX SDG Conference 2026, and mentored students in debating and Model United Nations.

As Chief Advisor of WMwrites, my vision is to help build a community where young writers can find their authentic voices, express their ideas with confidence, and engage thoughtfully with the world. I believe writing is more than an academic skill; it is a means of self-expression, empathy, and meaningful change. Through mentorship and creative exploration, I hope to nurture thoughtful, articulate, and socially conscious individuals who understand the power and responsibility of words.

Message from the Editorial Board

Message from Naranjan Das

In a region as linguistically rich as Sindh, language should be a bridge, never a barrier. That conviction drew me to WMwrites and its guiding principle of simple discourse: the belief that profound ideas are most meaningful when expressed with clarity, and that literature and linguistics should be accessible to every learner, not merely to those who have mastered the ornate registers of academia.

For over two and a half years, I have served as an English lecturer and IELTS instructor, guiding learners towards scholarly excellence and communicative confidence. I have also collaborated with writers across diverse disciplines, helping them refine their style and strengthen their arguments. I believe that good writing is not about complexity, but about precision, purpose, and empathy for the reader.

In this spirit, I am honoured to work with WMwrites to help nurture a community where language empowers, literature inspires, and every learner finds a voice worth sharing. I look forward to contributing to a body of work through which learners can read with understanding, write with confidence, and speak in a voice unmistakably their own.
