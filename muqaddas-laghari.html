Message from Muqaddas Laghari

As an English language educator and academic professional, my work focuses on teaching, academic coordination, and educational development. I hold an MA in Applied Linguistics from the University of Sindh and am currently pursuing an MPhil in Applied Linguistics to further strengthen my academic knowledge and research skills.

My academic interests include language education, teacher development, research, and the evolving role of artificial intelligence in education. I aspire to contribute to academic growth by connecting research with practical teaching, encouraging critical thinking, and supporting innovative approaches to teaching and learning.

As a Member of the Editorial Board at WMwrites, I look forward to contributing my academic knowledge and perspective to the development of thoughtful, credible, and meaningful educational content. I believe that clear and well-structured academic resources can support learners, encourage deeper understanding, and make a valuable contribution to the wider academic community.
